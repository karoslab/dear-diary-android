# Content rating notes

Fill the IARC / Play questionnaire as a **lifestyle journal**.

Suggested answers:

- Alcohol, tobacco, drugs: no
- Violence, weapons: no
- Sex, nudity: no
- Profanity: users may speak freely into their own journal; the app does not provide social features or user-generated public content
- Location sharing: no
- Digital purchases: none in this Android build (the iOS app has optional tips; this replica is free with no IAP)
- Users can communicate: no (there is no network and no chat)
- Users can share: only via the system share sheet when they export a backup they already own
- Age: Everyone / PEGI 3 equivalent (journal is personal, not a social network)

If the questionnaire asks whether users can create public UGC: **No**. Entries never leave the device unless the owner exports a file.
