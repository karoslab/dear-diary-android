## Full description

Speak, and your phone writes it down. Everything stays on this device. No account, no cloud, no upload. Turn on airplane mode and it keeps working.

dear diary is a voice journal with one rule: every word stays on this device.

Tap record and talk. Your phone turns your voice into text right there, using on-device Android speech. The entry is saved on your phone, with the audio and the words together. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account to make, no cloud to sync to, and no analytics watching you.

You can check the claim yourself. The app carries a “0 bytes sent” ledger, and the Android package has no internet permission, so journal audio and transcripts cannot leave through this app.

In the app:

- Record with pause and resume, and watch your words appear as you speak
- Every entry keeps its audio and its transcript together, and the transcript is yours to edit
- A journal timeline with a calendar heatmap and a streak count
- Search across everything you have said, run entirely on this device
- A gentle reflection prompt after each entry. Android has no Apple Intelligence, so the prompt is a fixed local prompt, and the app says so honestly
- Export everything as a single backup file, and import it back losslessly
- A wipe control that erases everything, because your journal is yours to destroy too
- Dark and light themes, and respect for your reduced motion setting

The speech model is packaged in the app (Vosk, English). There is no Google speech-model download. Your voice never leaves this phone.

dear diary collects nothing. There is nothing to sell, share, or leak, because nothing ever leaves your phone.
